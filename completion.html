Initiatives

THE PURPOSE

Academic setbacks and financial pressures can force students to abandon their schooling before completing high school. This initiative helps students overcome these barriers through focused examination coaching alongside vocational training, giving them an opportunity to complete their education while developing skills for employment.

BEYOND SETBACKS

Completing school while building skills for the future.

Students who have not passed their high school examinations are encouraged and supported to make another attempt while continuing their vocational training. This gives them an opportunity to complete their education without putting their skills development on hold.

Download Brochure

For students from underprivileged communities, not passing their high school examinations can bring their formal education to an early end. Many turn to vocational training to develop skills for employment. While these skills provide valuable opportunities, completing high school can further broaden their career prospects, improve earning potential and open doors to higher education and long-term growth.

THE PARTNERSHIP

To support this effort, Marek Foundation has partnered with Inter-Mission Industrial Development Association (IIDA), a nonprofit organisation dedicated to educating and empowering young people from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. Through its Skills Academy initiative, IIDA equips young women and men with industry-ready vocational skills and supports their journey towards employment.

HOW IT WORKS

Through this partnership, students receive focused coaching in the subjects they have not yet passed, alongside their vocational training. Classes are conducted for approximately two hours each afternoon, five days a week, helping them prepare to reappear for their pending subjects in the March board examinations.

THE IMPACT

By helping students complete their school education while continuing to develop vocational skills, Marek Foundation and IIDA work to keep educational opportunities within reach. This combined support enables young people to strengthen their qualifications, improve their employment prospects and build a more secure future.
